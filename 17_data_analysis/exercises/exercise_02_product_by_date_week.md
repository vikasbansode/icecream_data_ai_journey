# Exercise 02 — Best-Selling Product by Date and Week

## Problem
The product manager wants to know which ice cream sold the most units on a selected day and which product led each week.

## Tasks
1. Join fact sales with date and product dimensions.
2. For `2026-06-15`, rank products by units sold.
3. Calculate the best product for every week.
4. Return product name, units, and sales.
5. Explain the difference between ranking by units and ranking by sales amount.

## Files
`fact_sales.csv`, `dim_date.csv`, `dim_product.csv`.

## Student requirement
Use DuckDB SQL.
